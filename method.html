01COACH’S LENS

Evidence-first tactical prompts.

Live scores, match events, team statistics, and lineups can inform tactical prompts. The rules engine gives the reason beside each suggestion; it does not claim to observe player coordinates unless a tracking source is available.

  • Signals: possession, shots, corners, cards, substitutions, event timeline.
  • Example: reduce exposure on the side facing repeated wide entries.
  • Advice remains a prompt for a human coach to assess.
02DREAM LEAGUE

Build the model you can explain.

Lineup attributes are weighted by position into team attack, midfield, and defence scores. A probability layer estimates win, draw, and loss likelihoods. A separate 5-minute event simulation animates a plausible sample sequence.

  • Demo names and player stats are fictional.
  • Imported CSV values are user-provided and stay in this browser.
  • One simulated match is an entertainment/demo outcome, not a forecast of a real fixture.
03OPTIONAL LIVE DATA

Free-first connectors.

The free prototype runs in demo mode without keys. Vercel serverless endpoints can proxy optional developer credentials without placing them in public browser code.

  • API-Football documents a free tier with 100 requests per day. It can provide live fixtures, events, stats, and lineups; availability varies by competition.
  • YouTube Data API search has a default quota of 100 search calls per day. Clips play through YouTube’s official player or open on YouTube.
  • Player tracking coordinates and live broadcast video are not included in ordinary score/event feeds.

04REGIONAL BROADCAST LINKS

Country-aware, with a fallback.

On Vercel the app can read the request’s country code from Vercel’s IP-country header. Users can override the detected country. A provider is selected only when the competition and territory are mapped; otherwise the app opens the competition’s official broadcaster guide.

  • Premier League 2025/26–2027/28 lists JioStar for South Asia, Stan Sport for Australia, Fubo for Canada, and NBC Sports for the United States.
  • The ISL lists JioHotstar for all matches in India and OneFootball for many international territories.
  • Rights and exact match URLs can change; a provider home/official rights page is the fallback if no verified event deep-link is available.

WHAT THIS IS — AND ISN’T

Not a live video feed.

TOUCHLINE analyzes structured football data and user-selected clips. It does not restream sports broadcasts, bypass subscriptions, or promise every fixture has a free live feed. Any real-world stream opens at its official provider, where the provider handles its own availability and subscription access.
